Two worlds · one spa

Dogs get the party. Cats get the quiet.

The Dogs' World

Breed-specific cuts, custom styles, and a cage-free play space while they wait their turn at the table.

  • Breed & custom haircuts
  • Standard, medicated & flea baths
  • De-shedding for heavy coats
  • Puppy first grooms, gently introduced

The Cats' World

Slow hands, low voices, zero rush. Cats are groomed on their terms — even the most finicky felines.

  • Lion cuts — our specialty, 3.5–4 hours of detail
  • Detangling & gentle bathing
  • Nail trims in about ten minutes
  • Kitten first grooms, positively introduced

Nervous pet? That's our favourite kind — gaining their trust is the first service we offer, and it's free.
